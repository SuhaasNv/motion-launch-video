# Worked example: the PermitFlow showreel (20 s)

The film this skill was extracted from. Read `film.html` when you want a full 8-scene reference: the mark build, a
clipped diagonal wipe, a form card completing its sections, four documents scanned (one flagged), the flagged one carried into a
"fix only what was flagged" scene with an odometer and feedback states, a whip pan into a status timeline with a following camera
and an audit log, a zoom into the last node, a certificate landing with a stamp and sheen, and the end card.

`score.py` is its standalone score (it predates `scripts/score_engine.py`, which is the same instruments as a library).
Its fonts were the product's own files (Public Sans, Instrument Serif, IBM Plex Mono) placed next to the page.
The HUD overlay (`#hud`) is hidden: the owner asked for it to go.
